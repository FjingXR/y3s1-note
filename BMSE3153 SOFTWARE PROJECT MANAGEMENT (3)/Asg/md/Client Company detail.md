# 1(b) Project Client Profile and Motivation

## Project Client Profile

The client for this project is **LingJiaBan Co.**, formerly known as **CareerLink Recruitment Agency Co.** The company recently underwent a corporate rebranding initiative, changing its name from CareerLink Recruitment Agency Co. to LingJiaBan Co. while maintaining the same business operations, objectives, and organizational structure.

LingJiaBan Co. is a traditional Human Resource (HR) and headhunting agency operating nationwide across Malaysia. The company serves as an intermediary between employers and job seekers by helping organizations recruit qualified professionals while assisting individuals in finding suitable employment opportunities.

Prior to the emergence of modern online recruitment platforms, organizations commonly relied on agencies such as LingJiaBan Co. to source and screen potential employees. The agency's recruiters manually identified suitable candidates and connected them with employers seeking talent.

### Current Operations

At present, LingJiaBan Co. relies heavily on conventional offline processes and legacy workflows. These include:

1. Receiving recruitment requests from client companies seeking employees.
2. Searching through physical filing cabinets containing paper resumes submitted by job seekers.
3. Contacting candidates manually through telephone calls.
4. Recording recruitment progress using Microsoft Excel spreadsheets.
5. Sending resumes and recruitment updates to employers through email.

These manual procedures have become increasingly inefficient in a modern digital recruitment market.

### Current Business Issues

* Loss or misplacement of applicant information and resumes.
* Administrative delays caused by manual processing.
* Slow talent matching and recruitment procedures.
* Inefficient document storage and retrieval processes.
* Difficulty managing recruitment data across multiple staff members.
* Increased operational workload for HR personnel.
* Reduced competitiveness against modern online recruitment platforms.

### Market Position and Competitors

LingJiaBan Co. operates within the same recruitment industry as digital platforms such as JobStreet and LinkedIn. However, unlike these highly automated platforms, LingJiaBan Co. functions as a traditional headhunting agency where recruiters manually review resumes, contact candidates, and coordinate recruitment activities.

As recruitment technology continues to evolve, the company recognizes the need to modernize its operations to remain competitive within the Malaysian employment market.

## Motivation for Undertaking the Project

To address these challenges, LingJiaBan Co. intends to transform its operations into a digital recruitment agency by developing a centralized, web-based **Jobs Recruitment Website**.

The proposed system will consolidate recruitment activities into a single online platform and provide dedicated portals for different user groups:

### Job Seeker Portal

* Create and manage personal profiles.
* Upload resumes and supporting documents digitally.
* Search and apply for available job vacancies.

### Employer Portal

* Post job vacancies online.
* Review applicant submissions.
* Track recruitment progress through a centralized dashboard.

### Administrative Portal

* Manage candidate and employer information.
* Search resumes instantly using keyword-based filters.
* Schedule interviews digitally.
* Monitor recruitment activities and generate reports.

### Expected Benefits

The proposed system will:

* Digitize applicant and employer records.
* Eliminate excessive paperwork and manual data handling.
* Automate resume screening through keyword-based matching.
* Provide instant interview scheduling functionality.
* Improve communication between employers and job seekers.
* Increase recruitment efficiency and accuracy.
* Reduce operational turnaround time by more than **70%**.

By implementing the Jobs Recruitment Website, LingJiaBan Co. aims to modernize its traditional recruitment practices, improve operational performance, and compete more effectively with established online recruitment platforms while continuing to provide personalized recruitment services through its experienced human recruiters.
