Students will work in teams of 5 members per group (or any number deemed appropriate) from the
same tutorial class. Every member in the team is expected to contribute and participate actively in

3. Assignment Task

A  client  has  awarded  a software  project  to  your  team.  Now  your  team  is  required  to  write  a
report that shall include all the following content.
●  Project Background
●  Project Team
●  Project Scope
●  Project schedule
●  Quality Plan
●  Risk Table, etc

Notes:
●  You have to make an assumption about who the client is.
●  The  software  project  awarded  to  you  is  any  one  from  below  (i.e.,  choose  one  from  the
examples given in part 4), or your tutor may assign one to you. or you may propose your
own project title but subject to approval.

Refer to the “Appendix” for more details of the assignment tasks. Work as a team to carry out
the necessary research to complete this assignment. Additionally, each team member will take
turns  acting  as  project  manager  each  week  to  report  progress  to  the  tutor.  In  project
management,  a  project  manager’s  main  concern  is  to  complete  the  project  on  time,  within
budget, and to meet the required quality.

4. Project Schedule and Assumptions

Use Microsoft Project to create a schedule for the project timeline. List two assumptions you made

5. Quality Plan

Identify and justify one important software quality attribute for the system. For the quality attribute,

6. Risk Table

impact. Lastly, your risk mitigation, monitoring, and management plans.

7. Ethics and professional conduct for project management
a) Based on the Stakeholder Register, identify two strategies to engage key stakeholders

(specifically, the most influential/powerful stakeholder and potential end-users of the system)

b) Referring to the selected software process model, explain why it is also important to disclose the

disadvantages of the chosen model to relevant stakeholders.

c) If your project is significantly behind schedule, propose two corrective actions to bring the
project back on track. For each action, evaluate one advantage and one disadvantage.
