# **1.0 The Company**

## **1.1 Company Background**

Comet Retail Store Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian hypermarket retail company that provides a wide range of consumer products, including household items, groceries, electronic accessories, and daily necessities. The company operates 10 physical retail outlets across Sabah and Sarawak and also provides an online shopping platform through its website and mobile application.

The company combines physical retail operations with digital services to provide customers with a more convenient shopping experience. Customers can purchase products directly from retail outlets or place orders through the company’s online platform.

Due to the increasing demand for efficient retail services, Comet Retail Store relies on Information Technology systems to support its daily operations. The company’s IT environment supports important business activities such as sales transactions, inventory management, customer management, supplier coordination, and online order processing.

Comet Retail Store implements a centralized IT infrastructure at its main warehouse headquarters in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. The infrastructure manages important business applications and company data, allowing different retail branches, warehouse operations, employees, and online services to communicate and share information efficiently.

## **1.2 Business Processes**

Comet Retail Store performs several important business processes to support its daily retail operations. The main processes include sales transaction processing, inventory management, and online shopping services.

## **Sales Transaction Process**

The sales transaction process manages customer purchases at each retail outlet. Comet Retail Store uses a Point-of-Sale (POS) system to process transactions, record sales information, and generate receipts.

When a customer purchases a product, employees use the POS system to process the payment and record transaction details. The sales data is stored in the company database and the inventory information is updated automatically. This allows the company to maintain accurate sales records and monitor business performance.

## **Inventory Management Process**

The inventory management process is responsible for monitoring product availability across different branches and warehouses. The system tracks stock levels and helps employees manage product movement and restocking activities.

Accurate inventory information allows Comet Retail Store to reduce product shortages, maintain sufficient stock availability, and improve inventory planning.

## **Online Shopping Process**

Comet Retail Store provides online shopping services through its website and mobile application. Customers can browse products, place orders, make online purchases, and access delivery services.

The online shopping system is connected with the inventory system to ensure that product availability information is updated accurately. This helps prevent customers from purchasing unavailable products and improves the overall shopping experience.

## **1.3 Information Managed by the Company**

Comet Retail Store manages different types of business information to support daily operations, decision-making, and customer services.

## **Sales Information**

Sales information is generated from customer transactions through the POS system. It includes product details, transaction records, purchase amount, payment information, and store location.

This information allows management to monitor sales performance, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and develop better business strategies.

## **Inventory Information**

Inventory information is used to monitor product availability across branches and warehouses. It includes product details, stock quantity, and inventory status.

This information helps employees manage stock levels, arrange restocking activities, and ensure products are available for customers.

## **Customer Information**

Customer information is collected through the company’s online shopping platform and customer management system. It includes customer account details, order records, and delivery information.

This information helps Comet Retail Store improve customer services and provide a better shopping experience.

## **1.4 Technology Used**

To support its business operations, Comet Retail Store uses various technologies to manage transactions, store information, and provide digital services.

## **Point-of-Sale (POS) System**

The POS system is used at retail outlets to process customer purchases, record sales transactions, and generate receipts. It also connects with the inventory system to update stock information after each transaction.

## **Inventory Management System**

The inventory management system monitors product availability and manages stock information between different branches and warehouses. It helps employees track inventory levels and perform restocking activities efficiently.

## **Database Management System**

The database management system stores and manages important company information such as sales records, inventory data, and customer information. It allows the company to access and process business data efficiently.

## **Web and Mobile Application**

The company provides online shopping services through its website and mobile application. These platforms allow customers to browse products, place orders, and access digital shopping services.

## **1.5 IT Infrastructure Description**

Comet Retail Store implements a centralized IT infrastructure to support its retail operations. The main IT systems are managed at the company’s warehouse headquarters, where servers, storage systems, and network equipment support business applications and company data. The IT infrastructure consists of several important components:

## **Compute Infrastructure**

The company uses physical servers to run important business applications, including the POS system, inventory management system, and customer database. These servers provide the processing capability required for daily retail operations.

## **Storage Infrastructure**

Comet Retail Store uses storage systems to store important business data such as sales records, inventory information, customer details, and product information. Backup storage is also implemented to reduce the risk of data loss.

## **Network Infrastructure**

The network infrastructure connects different retail branches with the company headquarters. Technologies such as routers, switches, internet connections, and VPN connections allow branches to communicate with the central system and exchange business information.

## **End User Devices**

Employees use devices such as POS terminals, desktop computers, laptops, and barcode scanners to access company systems and perform daily tasks.

## **Security Infrastructure**

The company applies security controls such as user authentication, firewall protection, antivirus software, and CCTV monitoring to protect business systems and customer information.

Overall, Comet Retail Store’s IT infrastructure supports important retail activities by connecting business processes, information management, and technology systems. The infrastructure enables efficient sales processing, inventory monitoring, and online shopping services. As the company continues to expand, improvements to the existing IT infrastructure may be required to support higher business demands and maintain reliable system performance.
